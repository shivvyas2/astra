# Astrya typographic app icons

Created with the built-in image_gen tool. User direction: creatively write the full name Astrya and build the icon around the name and the app concept; preserve minimal brutalist styling.

## Astrya Matrix

Use case: logo-brand.
Asset type: ONE finished opaque square iOS / iPadOS app icon for Astrya, 1024×1024. Not a poster or mockup.
Creative brief: THE FULL APP NAME IS THE ICON. Make bespoke expressive typography for an app combining Vedic Hindu astrology, numerology, and Western planetary concepts. Minimal but brutalist: heavy confident letterforms, precise geometric construction, crisp flat cuts, excellent negative space. Scientific/celestial cues must be integrated into the lettering itself, never a generic symbol placed above a small app name.
Exact text: ASTRYA, spelled A-S-T-R-Y-A. All six letters must appear exactly once in that reading order, clearly recognizable. No substituted symbols in place of letters, no extra text, no captions or slogans.
Production: clean vector-like raster design, solid color fields, sharp anti-aliased edges, strong silhouette. Generous safe margins for an app icon, optically centered. Opaque background fills entire square with square outer corners, no pre-applied rounding.
Avoid: gradient, glow, shading, texture, grain, mockup, 3D, bevel, shadow, badge, small label, icons above/below the name, delicate fonts, illegible experimental typography, zodiac glyphs, tiny dots, extra stars, multiple options on the same image.
Composition and exact line breaks:
AST
RYA
Only two rows of three letters, top row AST and bottom row RYA, read left-to-right. Construct a strong compact almost-square wordmark filling roughly 68% of the canvas width and 60% height. Make each row the same width, carefully aligned on a hidden 3-column grid, with a narrow but clear horizontal gap between rows. No visible grid lines.
Custom lettering: extremely heavy architectural grotesk capitals, broad strokes and squared terminals. Each A is a broad truncated triangular form with a large circular negative-space counter and rectangular open base notch, but unambiguously readable as A. The R has a strong circular bowl counter and a sturdy diagonal leg. S, T, and Y must be very clear. The repeated perfect circular counters evoke celestial bodies while the six-letter modular arrangement feels mathematical. Letter strokes and the crossbars must remain substantial. This is a coherent designed typeface, not six random symbols.
Palette: exactly two colors, lime #DFEE6B lettering on an entirely flat near-black #0A0A0B background. Minimal, dense, ownable Astrya typographic emblem.
