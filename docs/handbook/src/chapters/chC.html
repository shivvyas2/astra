Appendix C

CGlossary and reading order

Every astrology term the code uses, every term the project coined, a path through the repository for a first week, and a table that turns "I want to change X" into a file and a chapter.

This appendix describes the code on main on 30 September 2026, version 0.3.0. The glossary is in two parts. The first is the astrological vocabulary, defined as the code uses it rather than as a textbook would, with the file where the term is computed or consumed. The second is the project's own vocabulary: words like "slot" and "moment" that mean something specific here. The reading order that follows starts at the chart engine and works outward to the phone, because that is the direction the data flows.

PadaA quarter of a nakshatra. Named in the first design note but not computed: the chart stores the nakshatra name only.Nowhere in the code
VimshottariThe 120-year dasha system: nine lords in a fixed order with fixed years (Ketu 7, Venus 20, Sun 6, Moon 10, Mars 7, Rahu 18, Jupiter 16, Saturn 19, Mercury 17). MahadashaA major period ruled by one lord, years long. The chart stores the current one; the timeline lays out all twelve from birth.computeVimshottari, vimshottariTimeline in dasha.ts
AntardashaA sub-period inside a mahadasha, nine per mahadasha in Vimshottari order from the mahadasha's own lord, each a proportional share. Laid out from the mahadasha's virtual start so it aligns to the period, not the birth moment.antardashaSpans in dasha.ts
DoshaAn affliction. In the code a Condition with a kind, a signature, a severity and a plain-sentence detail. Natal doshas are fixed at birth; transit afflictions come and go.lib/astrology/doshas.ts
Mangal doshaMars in house 1, 2, 4, 7, 8 or 12 from the ascendant, derived from the sign rather than the stored house number. Severity caution.detectNatalDoshas
Kaal Sarp yogaAll seven classical planets on one side of the Rahu to Ketu axis. Severity warning.detectNatalDoshas
Grahan doshaThe Sun or the Moon sharing a sign with Rahu or Ketu. One condition per luminary and node pair.detectNatalDoshas
Kemadruma yogaNo planet in the sign before or after the Moon, excluding the Sun and the nodes.detectNatalDoshas
Pitru doshaRahu or Ketu in the 9th house from the ascendant, derived from the sign. Severity info.detectNatalDoshas
Shrapit doshaSaturn and Rahu in the same sign.detectNatalDoshas

DignityHow strong a planet is by sign: one of exalted, moolatrikona, own, debilitated, neutral, in that precedence. Friendship is deliberately not a dignity.dignityOf in lib/astrology/derived.ts
ExaltedIn its sign of exaltation, with a deep degree recorded so the prompt can say "within a degree of exact". The nodes are never exalted.EXALTATION in derived.ts
DebilitatedIn the sign opposite its exaltation.dignityOf
MoolatrikonaA degree range inside one sign where a planet is nearly as strong as exalted. Where it shares the exaltation sign (Mercury in Virgo, the Moon in Taurus) the range takes precedence within its bounds.MOOLATRIKONA in derived.ts
Own signA sign the planet rules. Rahu and Ketu rule nothing.SIGN_LORD, signsRuledBy
CombustWithin a planet-specific orb of the Sun by the shorter arc; Mercury and Venus have tighter orbs when retrograde. The Sun and the nodes never combust.COMBUSTION, isCombust
Aspect, drishtiGraha drishti: every planet aspects the 7th house from itself; Mars adds the 4th and 8th, Jupiter the 5th and 9th, Saturn the 3rd and 10th; the nodes only the 7th. Rendered as "aspects houses" in the prompt and "linked to sections" on the phone.SPECIAL_ASPECTS, aspectedHouses

Mulank, bhagyank, namankThe root number from the birth day, the destiny number from the whole date, and the name number by the Pythagorean mapping, each reduced to one digit.computeNumerology, computeNameNumber in numerology.ts

C.2A reading order through the whole codebase

Twenty-six files, from the chart engine outward. Each takes a few minutes. Read them in order and the rest of the repository will be recognisable: every other file is a caller, a view or a test of one of these.

  1. lib/astrology/types.ts. The Chart shape everything else consumes. Note schemaVersion and the optional derived.
  2. lib/astrology/constants.ts. Signs, nakshatras, houseFrom, and CHART_SCHEMA_VERSION with its comment.
  3. lib/astrology/chart.ts. computeChart end to end: the flags, the two house rules, Ketu from Rahu, the derived block for Vedic only.
  4. lib/astrology/dasha.ts. Vimshottari from the Moon's longitude; the virtualStart comment explains the 0.1.0 fix.
  5. lib/astrology/derived.ts. The classical tables and the three things deliberately absent, in the header.
  6. lib/astrology/doshas.ts. The Condition type and why the signature exists; the natal and transit detectors.
  7. lib/astrology/transits.ts. The per-day transit cache and describeGochara, with the paragraph on why the old text was generic.
  8. lib/astrology/prompt.ts. The two halves, the Accuracy rules, and the Western fallback in renderChart.
  9. lib/anthropic.ts. Model names, adaptive thinking, and the three measured output figures.
  10. app/api/chat/route.ts. The whole reading path: profile, upgrade, transits, timeline block, history, two cache breakpoints, streaming, the usage log line.
  11. docs/MODEL_COSTS.md. What a turn costs and the four changes that made it cheaper.
  12. lib/supabase/route.ts. Bearer or cookie, in thirty lines.
  13. lib/env.ts. Which variables are required, which are optional, and the server-only import that keeps them there.
  14. supabase/migrations/0001_init.sql. The five original tables, the is_admin function, and the owner-only policies.
  15. lib/data/birthProfile.ts. Intake writes both charts; ensureCurrentChart upgrades on read and swallows a failed write on purpose.
  16. lib/alerts/slots.ts then lib/alerts/predictions.ts. The local-clock rule, the unique index as the safety net, the fallback reading.
  17. lib/alerts/run.ts. The signature diff, partitionForAlerts, and the comments that forbid filtering the raw sets.
  18. app/api/cron/daily/route.ts with vercel.json. Two schedules, one secret, two jobs in sequence.
  19. lib/timeline/build.ts. The life map: periods, moments placed by date, the events hash, the now row.
  20. mobile/project.yml. Targets, entitlements, the files shared with the widget, and the comment on each.
  21. mobile/Sanchara/Sources/Services/Supabase.swift then SancharaAPI.swift. The one client, the bearer header, and one method per route.
  22. mobile/Sanchara/Sources/Features/Intake/ProfileStore.swift. How the chart reaches the phone: read under RLS, cached, usable offline.
  23. mobile/Sanchara/Sources/Services/ChartCache.swift. Why three processes share one file, and what never goes in it.
  24. mobile/Sanchara/Sources/Features/Chat/ChartFacts.swift then OnDeviceReasoner.swift. The neutral table and the three load-bearing rules for the on-device model.
  25. mobile/Sanchara/Sources/Intents/SancharaIntents.swift and DeepLink.swift. What Siri can answer and how a process with no UI tells the app where to land.
  26. mobile/SancharaWidgets/ReadingWidget.swift. A widget that never fetches, and the twice-daily refresh boundary.

C.4Study notes

Read in this order

  1. The glossary's astrology table against lib/astrology/doshas.ts, checking each named dosha against its detector.
  2. Files 1 to 7 of the reading order with their .test.ts neighbours open; the test names read as the rules.
  3. The rest of the reading order, one stage a day.

Check yourself

Which glossary term is named in a design note but computed nowhere?

Pada. The chart stores only the nakshatra name from nakshatraOf.

What is the difference between a condition and an alert?

A condition is a computed fact with a signature, recorded in transit_conditions whether or not anyone is told. An alert is the composed notification written to alerts only when a notifiable condition starts or ends.

Where does a Siri intent get the chart, and what happens if it is not there?

From ChartCache in the App Group, via IntentData.chart(). If nothing is cached the intent says so in words rather than throwing, because "an intent that cannot find data says so in words, which is a better outcome than an error Siri reads out as a failure."

You want to add a Western technique. Which file, and what must you not do?

A new module beside derived.ts, gated on chart.tradition === "western". You must not extend deriveFacts or factsFor, which are Vedic by design and return nothing for a tropical chart.
